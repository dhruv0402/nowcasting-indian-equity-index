## 1. Mathematical Framework & Seismic Calibration

### 1.1 The Null Hypothesis of Random Walk Drift
Let $P(t)$ denote the continuous 1-minute midpoint price series of an asset $S$. Under the null hypothesis $H_0$ of efficient price discovery without external information shock:

$$dP(t) = \mu P(t)dt + \sigma P(t)dW(t)$$

Over any observation horizon $t \in [0, \tau]$ following a news event at $t=0$, the maximum expected random walk price displacement under standard Brownian motion is bounded by:

$$\Delta P_{\text{max}}(t) = 2.0 \cdot \sigma \sqrt{t}$$

Any return shock satisfying $|\Delta P(t)| < 2.0\sigma\sqrt{t}$ is rigorously classified as **Null Baseline Tremor (Noise)**.

### 1.2 The Richter Financial Magnitude Scale
To normalize information shocks across heterogeneous asset classes (e.g., Micro-Caps vs Mega-Cap Blue-Chips vs Crypto), we define the **Logarithmic Richter Shock Magnitude ($M_R$)**:

$$M_R = \log_{10} \left( \frac{|R_{0 \to t}|}{\sigma_{\text{15m baseline}}} + 1 \right) \times \gamma_{\text{category}}$$
